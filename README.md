# Monetary Economics

</p>

This course provides an in-depth study of money, commercial banking, and monetary-fiscal policy. Students will gain insights into the role of money, how interest rates are determined, central banking and its economic impacts both domestically and internationally, as well as the role of fiscal policy in price level determination. As an extension to these core topics, this course will discuss how blockchain technology is revolutionizing the financial sector. To optimize learning outcomes, this course incorporates artificial intelligence to enhance students' understanding of both traditional monetary systems and innovative blockchain technologies. These AI tools provide personalized learning, real-time feedback, and practical application support, preparing students to navigate the rapidly evolving financial world.
